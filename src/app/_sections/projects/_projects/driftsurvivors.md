A mobile game inspired by Vampire Survivors. Drift around a desert, run over enemies, and shoot them with a variety of weapons. Try to survive as long as you can against increasingly strong waves of enemies.

I built it with Godot and custom pixel art, with a structure that makes it easy to add new weapons and upgrades.

You can download and play the game on Android from the [Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=dev.clotet.driftsurvivors).
