A web app to manage [Secret Santa](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secret_Santa) groups with friends and family. I built it as a prototype, with plans to rewrite it later.

You can create groups, invite people through a link or your phone's share dialog, randomly assign each person's Secret Santa, and share a wish list to give them gift ideas.
